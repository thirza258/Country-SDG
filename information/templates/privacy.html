Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. Learn how SDG Country Profiles protects your privacy on our self-hosted infrastructure.

1. Self-Hosted Infrastructure Guarantee

SDG Country Profiles is an independently operated, fully self-hosted data portal. All Sustainable Development Goal indicator caches, published reports, country profiles, and search requests are processed and retained exclusively on our private self-hosted servers. Your data does not leave our secured perimeter for tracking, profiling, or monetization.

2. AI Features & Third-Party Processing (OpenRouter)

When available, our optional AI country narrative insights and trend syntheses are powered by language models accessed through OpenRouter.

When you view an AI-generated summary of a country's SDG progress, structured metric snapshots are dispatched to OpenRouter API endpoints for evaluation. No personal identifiers or account details are sent to OpenRouter. Use of AI features is subject to:

  • OpenRouter Privacy Policy →
  • OpenRouter Terms of Service →

3. Information We Collect

  • Server Access Logs: Standard web server connection logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) retained temporarily for security and anomaly detection.
  • Client Storage: Minimal session cookies or local preference flags to remember search preferences and theme choices.

4. Contact

For privacy questions or data inquiries regarding this self-hosted service, please contact us at nevatalapp@gmail.com.
